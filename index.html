How funding works

Ways visits can be covered

Support at Home

Social support and transport are Support at Home services, so visits can often be paid from your budget. Ask your provider to add us, or we'll talk to them for you. Support at Home replaced Home Care Packages on 1 November 2025.

DVA & community programs

Veterans and eligible community-program clients may have funded in-home support.

Private health insurers

Some insurers fund companion or post-hospital support for eligible policyholders.

Employer benefit

If your workplace partners with us, visits are billed straight to your employer.

An ongoing commitment

Safety before, during and after every visit

Police & background checks

The most thorough checks the law allows, before a companion can join the platform. A person reviews every result, not only software, and checks are repeated every year with monitoring in between.

  • National police check
  • Working with Children Check (where needed)
  • NDIS worker screening (where needed)
  • Identity match
  • Reference checks

Companion identity verification

Photo ID is checked and matched to a live selfie, so we know every companion is exactly who they say they are.

Education & onboarding

Every companion completes our training program before their first visit, and agrees to our Visit Standards before each one they accept.

Online experience for Seekers and families

Families can see visit details, including their companion's name, photo and car, at any time in the family portal.

Visit safety

Support during every visit, so Seekers and companions both feel safe:

  • Digital ID badges in the app, so Seekers can confirm who's at the door
  • Masked phone numbers that protect both sides and can be switched off any time
  • Emergency help in the companion app, answered by a real person
  • Visit check-in and check-out, and continuous review of visit feedback

Visit Standards

What every companion agrees to

  • Arrive on time and show their ID at the door
  • Stick to the help that was booked, and check before doing anything else
  • Keep personal and health details private
  • Never handle money beyond agreed errands, and never accept gifts
  • Never give medication or hands-on medical care
  • Report any concern about wellbeing the same day

Community Standards

Community Standards

When you become a companion or welcome one into your home, you help build the world we want: one where nobody has to go it alone. Kindness, integrity and safety are the foundation of every interaction.

Kindness: we choose kindness

We try to be a pal in everything we do. We go above and beyond with care and compassion, and show empathy and understanding.

Integrity: we do what's right

We keep our word and trust each other with personal information. We show up when and where we say we will, and hold ourselves accountable.

Safety: we put safety first

We look after one another. We never put anyone's safety at risk, and we treat others as we'd like to be treated.

Breaking the standards

Reports are investigated by our team. Depending on what happened, the outcome can range from a warning to permanent removal from the platform, and we report to police where required.

Terms & Conditions

By registering for or using Any Help Any Time's services, you agree to these terms. Services are booked through registered accounts and paid for according to the pricing shown on this site at the time of booking.

Cancellations and rescheduling should be made with reasonable notice; our team will confirm the notice period that applies to your package. Companions are engaged and screened by Any Help Any Time; ratings left by families and elders help us maintain quality but do not form part of any legal warranty.

We reserve the right to decline or pause a service where a request falls outside what our companions are trained or authorized to provide, including medical procedures beyond routine health checks.

Privacy Policy

We collect the information you provide when registering (name, contact details, and any care notes you choose to share) to arrange and deliver services. Companion applicants' information is used to review and manage applications.

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the companions assigned to a visit and with staff who need it to run the service. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us.

Health-related notes (such as mobility needs or health-check results) are treated as sensitive information and are only shared with the companions directly involved in a visit.
